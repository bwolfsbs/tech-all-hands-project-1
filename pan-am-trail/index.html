THE PAN-AM TRAIL

USHUAIA, ARGENTINA → PRUDHOE BAY, ALASKA — 25,300 KM

a loving homage to a certain 8-bit wagon game

HOW TO PLAY

Drive the entire Pan-American Highway from the bottom of the world to the top. Keep your crew of five alive: manage money, fuel, food, spare parts, and morale.

KEYBOARD: number keys pick menu options · ENTER confirms / pauses & resumes travel · on the road: R rest, F forage, P pace, N rations, S status.

MOUSE: click anything. In the forage minigame, click food. Do not click the snake.

Diseases are real out here: dysentery, dengue, norovirus, altitude sickness. Gear helps — a water filter, bug spray, a satellite phone.

At the Darién Gap the road simply stops. You will have to make a choice. People have regretted all three.
